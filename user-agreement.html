User Agreement

Effective 1 November 2026

This agreement covers your use of Disc, a personal disc-golf round tracker. By using Disc you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

What Disc does

Disc lets you build a round, log throws hole by hole, compare them with a par reference, note a miss cause, and render a vertical reel from the scorecard and the clips you add. Scores are a personal record. They are not an official competition result, a ranking, or a certified measurement. Disc does not provide GPS distances, course maps, bookings, tickets, or equipment sales.

Sample course cards are labeled Sample. They show a layout you can copy into a round. They are not an offer to play a specific course and they do not reserve a tee time.

Sign-in

You open Disc with one tap after you agree to this agreement and the Privacy Policy. Disc does not offer a password or Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session and keeps your rounds, reels, and credits on this iPhone. Delete Account removes the account and those records from this iPhone and cannot be undone. Removing the account does not refund charges already made by Apple.

Your content

Notes, scores, clips, and reels stay in the app container on your device. You are responsible for the material you record or import. Do not import media you do not have the right to use.

Credits

A preview does not spend credits. When you confirm Export Reel, Disc spends the stated number of Disc Reel Credits only after the MP4 is written and checked. A standard export spends 1 credit. A dual-page or 4K export spends 2 credits. Credits are consumable, sold by Apple, and stored on this device. They cannot be restored after the app is removed or after you clear all data. Unused credits from a revoked purchase are removed; finished reels are not deleted for that reason.

Permissions

Camera, photo library, microphone, and speech recognition are requested only when you tap the control that needs them. You can keep scoring if you decline.

Availability

Disc is provided as is. A failed export does not spend credits. You can retry. Disc may not be available if the device is out of storage or if Apple's purchase services are unavailable.
