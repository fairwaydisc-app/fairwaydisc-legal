Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Disc is a disc-golf round tracker. It keeps scorecards, throw notes, clips, voiceovers, and reel-export credits on the device where you install it. Disc does not operate an account system and does not upload your rounds to a Disc server.

Data stored on this device

When you start a round, Disc saves the course description, hole count, par reference, hole scores, miss attributions, and notes in an on-device database. Clips you film, photos and videos you import, voiceovers, rendered MP4 reels, and cover images are stored in the app's private folder. A local ledger stores consumable Disc Reel Credits, including a small starter balance and credits added after a verified App Store purchase.

Course cards are bundled layouts. They are not live course reservations, tickets, or a social feed. Choosing one stores that choice with the round.

Camera

Disc asks for the camera only after you tap Film Throw, to record a throw or line clip for the current hole. The recording uses video only. If you decline, the round stays, and you can import a clip or log the hole without one.

Photo library

Import uses the system photo picker after you tap Import or Import from Photos. Disc does not request full photo-library read access for that picker. Selected photos or videos are copied into the round. Disc asks to add to your photo library only after you tap Save to Photos, to write the finished reel and cover. Declining a save leaves the reel in the app so you can try again. Imported copies do not change the original items in your library.

Microphone and speech recognition

Disc asks for the microphone only after you tap Record Voiceover or the microphone button beside a note field. Speech recognition, when you use that microphone button, turns speech into text for the field you are editing. Recognition is requested on device when the device supports it. If you decline, you can still type the note, and a reel can still be exported without a voiceover.

Purchases

Reel exports are paid with consumable Disc Reel Credits through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. Disc stores the resulting credit balance on this device. Credits are not an account balance, are not restored after you remove the app or clear all data, and are not transferred to another device. Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple revokes a purchase, Disc removes only the unused part of that grant. Reels that were already created stay on the device.

Notifications

After you reach the main tabs, Disc asks Apple's notification prompt. If you allow notifications, Disc registers for remote notifications and stores the device token on this iPhone. Delete Account and Clear All Data remove that token. Any advertising identifier saved by an earlier version is removed at the same time and is not requested again.

What Disc does not do

  • Disc does not ask you to create a password or use Sign in with Apple.
  • Disc does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not request App Tracking Transparency.
  • Disc does not sell your rounds, clips, or notes.

Sign-in on this device

Disc signs you in with one tap after you agree to this policy and the User Agreement. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The session stays on this iPhone. Disc does not run an account server.

Log Out ends the session. Rounds, reels, and credits stay on this iPhone. You tap Continue again to open them.

Delete Account removes the account stored on this iPhone and the rounds, hole records, reels, and credits saved with it. That deletion cannot be undone. It does not refund a purchase Apple has already charged.

Clearing data

Clear All Data in Settings deletes rounds, hole records, clips, reels, course-card choices, and the remaining credit balance from this install. You can also remove the app to delete its sandbox. Clear All Data does not sign you out by itself. Delete Account both removes those records and ends the account on this iPhone.
